# 🌿 Moodli Admin Web 

> A cozy, Ghibli-inspired admin dashboard and business planner designed specifically for **Moodli** (our charm & keychain studio). Built to make managing business metrics, tracking inventory, and organizing studio payroll feel as relaxing as a walk in a secret mossy garden.

Live Demo of Moodli: [moodli.netlify.app](https://moodli.netlify.app)

---

## 🍃 The Vibe & Aesthetic
Traditional corporate dashboards are cold, gray, and stressful. **Moodli Admin Web** reimagines business management through a **Ghibli Greenery** lens:
* **Color Palette:** Soft sage greens, mossy tones, warm cedarwood accents, and gentle cream backdrops.
* **Feel:** Organic, clean, and interactive. Smooth hover transitions, rounded corners, and cozy, friendly iconography.
* **Purpose:** To make the "not-so-fun" parts of running a small business (like spreadsheets, taxes, and cost-tracking) genuinely delightful.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 📊 Cozy Dashboard Overview
* **Quick Metrics:** Instant snapshots of *Total Sales This Month*, *Net Profit (Kita)*, *Active Crew*, and *Next Studio Event*.
* **Visual Ledger:** A simplified, beautiful chart tracking Revenue vs. Expenses so you always know where your studio stands financially.
* **Reminders Inbox:** Gentle notifications for low stock, upcoming paydays, or pending tasks.

### 2. 👥 Employee & Payroll Manager
* Easily log your studio helpers, packaging crew, and designers.
* Set custom rates (hourly or per-piece) and track payment statuses with aesthetic tags.

### 3. 🗓️ Interactive "Weekly Ganap" Planner
* **Studio Calendar:** Map out your upcoming physical pop-up markets, restocks, and major production deadlines.
* **Weekly Ganap Suggestions:** An interactive sidebar that acts as your cozy business partner! It dynamically suggests seasonal trends, marketing ideas, or content prompts (e.g., *"Behind-the-Scenes Packing ASMR"*). 
* **"Adopt Event" Feature:** One-click integration to instantly turn a suggested "ganap" into an active event on your schedule.

### 4. 💸 Budget & Profit Ledger (Kita vs. Gastos)
* Input your product inflows (sales) and outflows (booth fees, clay, acrylic blanks, shipping bags).
* **Automatic Net Profit Calculator:** Instantly deducts total expenses from your total revenue.

### 5. 📦 Raw Materials & Inventory Monitor
* Track essential supplies like Acrylic Blanks, Metal Keyrings, Tassels, Sticker Paper, and Mailers.
* Visual progress bars that shift color to alert you when raw materials are running dangerously low.

### 6. 🧮 Smart COGS & Pricing Calculator
* An interactive mini-calculator to help price products correctly.
* Simply input materials, packaging, and labor costs, and the app will calculate the actual **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** and suggest a healthy retail price based on a 60% margin.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

This project is built using:
* **Frontend:** React.js / Vite (or HTML5 + Tailwind Play)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (configured with custom Ghibli-inspired hex codes)
* **Icons:** Lucide React (for clean, organic, and friendly icons)
* **State Management:** Local State (React Hooks) for seamless real-time interactivity on the preview.
